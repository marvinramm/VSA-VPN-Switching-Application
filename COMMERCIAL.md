VSA is licensed under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE.md).
Private and other noncommercial use, including modifying and further developing the app, is free.

**Any commercial use requires a separate written agreement with the author *before* you start.**

## What counts as commercial use?

Examples, not a complete list:

- Selling VSA or an app based on it (e.g. a paid app in an app store)
- Monetizing VSA or a derivative through ads, in-app purchases, subscriptions or donations tied to features
- Bundling VSA or parts of its code into a paid product or service
- Using VSA within a company, or offering it as part of paid services for customers (e.g. IT service providers, MDM rollouts)
- Any other use where you or your organization earn money with VSA or its code

If you are unsure whether your use is commercial, ask first.

## How it works

Commercial licenses are arranged individually. Depending on the use case, the options are:

- **Individual agreement**, e.g. a one-time or yearly license fee
- **Revenue share**, a percentage of the revenue made with VSA or a product based on it
